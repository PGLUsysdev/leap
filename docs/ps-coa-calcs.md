# 📋 Full Documentation: Government Salaries, Allowances, and Benefits (2026 Update)

> **Scope**: All 26 account codes from 5-01-01-010 through 5-01-04-990, re-verified against the latest DBM, BIR, GSIS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and COA issuances as of 2026.
> **Applicable to**: National Government Agencies (NGAs), Local Government Units (LGUs) including the **Provincial Government of La Union (PGLU)** , and other covered entities.

---

## 🔍 Overview

This document compiles the latest formulas, rates, and guidelines for all salary and benefit accounts used in Philippine government accounting. The rates reflect the **Third Tranche** of the Salary Standardization Law under **Executive Order No. 64, s. 2024**, effective **January 1, 2026**, as implemented through **National Budget Circular No. 601** (for national government agencies) and **Local Budget Circular No. 172** (for local government personnel).

A final **Fourth Tranche** is scheduled for **January 1, 2027**.

## 💰 Part I: Salaries and Wages

### 5-01-01-010 — Salaries and Wages – Regular

**Formula**: Monthly salary rate per the **Third Tranche Monthly Salary Schedule** under EO No. 64, s. 2024, effective January 1, 2026.

**Key Updates (2026)** :

- The Third Tranche was implemented via **National Budget Circular No. 601** dated January 22, 2026, for national government agencies.
- For LGUs, the corresponding guidelines are in **Local Budget Circular No. 172**, issued in July 2026.
- Entry-level (SG-1, Step 1) salary increased to **₱14,634** effective January 1, 2026.
- Example: An Administrative Officer II (SG-11) earning ₱30,024 as of December 31, 2025 received an adjusted salary of **₱31,705** effective January 1, 2026.
- The final **Fourth Tranche** will take effect **January 1, 2027**.

### 5-01-01-020 — Salaries and Wages – Casual/Contractual

**Formula**:

```
Daily Wage Rate = Authorized Monthly Salary ÷ 22 days
Actual Pay = Daily Wage Rate × Days Actually Worked
```

**Notes**: The monthly salary is derived from the same Salary Schedule used for regular employees. Casual and contractual personnel are covered by NBC No. 601 and LBC No. 172.

## 🧾 Part II: Allowances and Benefits

### 5-01-02-010 — Personnel Economic Relief Allowance (PERA)

**Rate**: **₱2,000 per month** for full-time employees.

**Part-time/Absence Formula**:

```
PERA per day = ₱2,000 ÷ 22 working days = ₱90.91/day
```

**2026 Update**: The 2027 National Expenditure Program includes a proposed ₱500 increase, which would bring PERA to **₱2,500 per month** if approved. However, as of 2026, the rate remains at **₱2,000**.

### 5-01-02-020 & 5-01-02-030 — Representation Allowance (RA) and Transportation Allowance (TA)

**Legal Basis**: **DBM Local Budget Circular No. 157, s. 2024**, effective for FY 2024 and years thereafter.

**Coverage** (LGU Officials):

1. Local Chief Executives (LCEs)
2. Vice-LCEs
3. Members of the Local Sanggunian (including Ex-Officio Members)
4. Department Heads
5. Assistant Department Heads
6. Chiefs of Hospital
7. Division Chiefs in Special Cities

**Proration Formula for Leave**:

| Number of Workdays of Actual Work Performance in a Month | Actual RATA for a Month  |
| :------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------- |
| 1 to 5 days                                              | **25%** of monthly RATA  |
| 6 to 11 days                                             | **50%** of monthly RATA  |
| 12 to 16 days                                            | **75%** of monthly RATA  |
| 17 or more days                                          | **100%** of monthly RATA |

_Source:_ LBC No. 157, Section 6.0.

**Note**: The specific monthly rates for each position are in **Annex B** of LBC No. 157 and vary by rank and LGU income class. No amount exceeding the authorized rates may be granted. If funds are insufficient, RATA may be granted partially at a uniform percentage.

### 5-01-02-040 — Clothing/Uniform Allowance

**Rate**: Up to **₱8,000 per annum** (non-taxable ceiling).

**Legal Basis**: **BIR Revenue Regulations No. 29-2025**, effective **January 6, 2026**, which increased the non-taxable ceiling from ₱7,000 to **₱8,000 per annum**.

**Notes**: The allowance is granted to qualified government personnel occupying regular, contractual, or casual positions. For part-time personnel, the allowance is prorated based on service rendered.

### 5-01-02-050 — Subsistence Allowance

**Rates**:

- **Public Health Workers (PHWs)** : At least **₱50.00 per day** of work.
- **Military and Uniformed Personnel** : **₱350.00 per day** effective January 1, 2026 (increased from ₱150).

**Legal Basis**: For PHWs, this is mandated under RA 7305 (Magna Carta for Public Health Workers). For uniformed personnel, it is governed by the FY 2026 GAA and EO No. 107, s. 2025.

### 5-01-02-060 — Laundry Allowance

**Rates**:

- **Public Health Workers (PHWs)** : **₱150.00 per month** for those who render actual service on all workdays in a month.
- **General non-taxable ceiling** (BIR): **₱400.00 per month** effective January 6, 2026, per RR No. 29-2025.

**Formula for PHWs (partial month)** :

```
LA = (₱500 / 1 month) × (1 month / 22 workdays) × D days of actual service
   = ₱22.727 × D days
```

### 5-01-02-070 — Quarters Allowance

**Classification**: **Optional** (for most LGU personnel).

**Rate**: Determined by the agency head and subject to specific guidelines. Often varies by rank for uniformed personnel. There is no standardized national rate; it depends on the agency's policy and budget availability.

### 5-01-02-080 — Productivity Incentive Allowance

**Classification**: **Optional**.

**Rate**: A fixed amount granted to authorized officials/employees for at least satisfactory performance. Not a fixed formula but a specific amount determined by the agency's budget and guidelines, typically requiring an ordinance from the Sanggunian.

### 5-01-02-090 — Overseas Allowance

**Classification**: **Mandatory** (for covered foreign service personnel).

**Rate**: Adjusts the take-home pay of foreign service personnel for changes in the cost of living abroad, based on the post's index. Personnel assigned to hardship posts may receive an additional **5% higher rate**.

### 5-01-02-100 — Honoraria

**Classification**: **Optional**.

**Formula**:

```
Honorarium = (0.023) × (MSR) × (T)
```

Where:

- **MSR** = Monthly Salary Rate
- **T** = Total actual lecture/training hours + preparation hours

**Cap**: The total honoraria received from all special projects shall not exceed **25% of the annual basic salary**.

**Coverage**: Honoraria may be paid to teaching personnel, lecturers, resource persons, coordinators, and facilitators.

### 5-01-02-110 — Hazard Pay

**Classification**: **Mandatory** (for Public Health Workers under RA 7305).

**Rates**:

| Salary Grade    | Hazard Pay Rate                          |
| :-------------- | :--------------------------------------- |
| SG-19 and below | At least **25%** of monthly basic salary |
| SG-20 and above | **5%** of monthly basic salary           |

**Legal Basis**: RA 7305 (Magna Carta for Public Health Workers) and its Implementing Rules.

**Note**: PHWs exposed to high-risk hazards may receive hazard pay **not exceeding 5% higher** than the prescribed rates. Under the proposed Magna Carta for Barangay Health Workers, volunteer BHWs may receive hazard allowance, subject to legislative approval.

### 5-01-02-120 — Longevity Pay

**Classification**: **Optional** for most employees; **Mandatory** only for specific positions (e.g., Judiciary, S&T personnel).

**Formulas**:

- **Justices and Judges**: **5% of monthly basic pay** for each five (5) years of continuous, efficient, and meritorious service.
- **S&T Personnel**: **5% of current monthly basic salary** for every 5 years of continuous and meritorious service, per RA 8439.
- **Other government employees**: May be computed at **10% of monthly basic pay** for every five years of continuous satisfactory service.

**Rule**: Those already enjoying longevity pay are **no longer entitled to step increment**.

### 5-01-02-130 — Overtime and Night Pay

#### Overtime Pay

**Formula**:

```
OT Pay = Basic Hourly Rate × 125% × Number of OT Hours
```

Where **Basic Hourly Rate = Monthly Salary ÷ 22 ÷ 8**.

**Example** (2026 rate for SG-15): Hourly rate = ₱54.38; OT rate = ₱54.38 × 125% = **₱67.98/hour** on regular days.

#### Night Shift Differential (NSD)

**Formula**:

```
NSD = 10% of Regular Wage × Number of Hours Worked between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM
```

**Legal Basis**: NSD is **10% of the regular wage** for each hour of work performed between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM.

**Note**: For government personnel occupying positions from Division Chief and below, NSD may be paid at a rate **not exceeding 20%** of the hourly basic rate, as determined by the head of the agency. For public health workers, NSD shall not be lower than **10%** of their hourly basic rate.

### 5-01-02-140 — Year End Bonus

**Formula**:

```
Year-End Bonus = 1 month basic pay as of October 31 of the current year
```

**Legal Basis**: RA No. 6686, as amended by RA No. 8441; DBM Budget Circular No. 2016-4, as amended by BC No. 2024-3.

**Proration Based on Length of Service** (January 1 to October 31):

| Length of Service               | % of Basic Pay |
| :------------------------------ | :------------- |
| 4 months but less than 5 months | 50%            |
| 3 months but less than 4 months | 40%            |
| 2 months but less than 3 months | 30%            |
| 1 month but less than 2 months  | 20%            |
| Less than 1 month               | 10%            |

**Eligibility**: Must have rendered at least **four (4) months of aggregate service** from January 1 to October 31 of the year.

**Release**: Not earlier than **November 15** of the current year.

### 5-01-02-150 — Cash Gift

**Rate**: **₱5,000** (fixed amount).

**Legal Basis**: RA No. 6686, as amended by RA No. 8441. Granted along with the Year-End Bonus.

**Proration**: Same percentage rates as the Year-End Bonus based on length of service.

**2026 Update**: The 2026 guidelines confirm that the Year-End Bonus and Cash Gift remain governed by DBM BC No. 2016-4, as amended by BC No. 2024-3, and apply to NGAs, SUCs, GOCCs, and LGUs.

### 5-01-02-990 — Other Bonuses and Allowances

**Classification**: **Optional**.

**Coverage**: This account records other authorized bonuses and allowances not falling under any specific compensation account, such as:

- **Performance-Based Bonus (PBB)** : Subject to IATF guidelines and agency performance targets.
- **Collective Negotiation Agreement (CNA) Incentive**: Requires a valid CNA between the LGU and the employees' union, and is subject to DBM rules and the PS limitation.
- **Anniversary Bonus**, **Loyalty Incentive**, and other incentives as may be categorized by DBM.

## 📊 Part III: Personnel Benefit Contributions

### 5-01-03-010 — Retirement and Life Insurance Premiums

**Contribution Rates** (GSIS, RA 8291):

| Contributor           | Rate                            |
| :-------------------- | :------------------------------ |
| Employee              | **9%** of monthly basic salary  |
| Government (Employer) | **12%** of monthly basic salary |
| **Total**             | **21%**                         |

**Key Features**:

- **No salary ceiling** — the contribution is based on actual basic monthly salary.
- **No MSC brackets** — unlike SSS, GSIS does not round to the nearest bracket.
- The contribution base includes **basic monthly salary only** — PERA, honoraria, overtime, hazard add-ons, per diems, and one-off bonuses **do not** feed the 9% deduction.

### 5-01-03-020 — Pag-IBIG Contributions

**Contribution Rates** (Pag-IBIG Fund Circular No. 460):

| Monthly Fund Salary | Employee Rate | Employer Rate | Total |
| :------------------ | :------------ | :------------ | :---- |
| ₱1,500 and below    | **1%**        | **2%**        | 3%    |
| Over ₱1,500         | **2%**        | **2%**        | 4%    |

**Maximum Fund Salary (MFS)** : **₱10,000 per month**.

**Employer Share for Government Employees**: The employer share of **₱200 per month per employee** is funded from the **Miscellaneous Personnel Benefits Fund (MPBF)** for NGAs, effective February 2024.

### 5-01-03-030 — PhilHealth Contributions

**Contribution Rate for 2026**: **5%** of monthly basic salary (no hike from 2025).

**Premium Computation**:

| Monthly Basic Salary         | Monthly Premium          |
| :--------------------------- | :----------------------- |
| ₱10,000 (floor)              | ₱500 (fixed)             |
| ₱10,000.01 to ₱99,999.99     | **5%** of monthly income |
| ₱100,000 and above (ceiling) | ₱5,000 (maximum)         |

**Sharing**: For employed members, the premium is **shared equally** between employee and employer (**2.5% each**).

**Legal Basis**: RA No. 11223 (Universal Health Care Act).

### 5-01-03-040 — Employees Compensation Insurance Premiums

**Rate**: **1% of annual basic salary** or **₱1,200.00 per annum**, whichever is lower.

**Legal Basis**: P.D. 626 (Employees Compensation and State Insurance Fund).

### 5-01-03-050 — Provident/Welfare Fund Contributions

**Classification**: **Optional**.

**Rate**: Not a standardized national government contribution. Depends on the specific provident or welfare fund established by the LGU. Contributions are typically a percentage of basic salary, as defined by the fund's own rules and the LGU's ordinance.

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Part IV: Retirement and Other Benefits

### 5-01-04-010 — Pension Benefits

**Formula** (GSIS, RA 8291):

```
BMP = (0.025) × (AMC + ₱700) × (PPP)
```

Where:

- **BMP** = Basic Monthly Pension
- **AMC** = Average Monthly Compensation for the last three years
- **PPP** = Period with Paid Premiums (in months)

**Minimum Pension**: The monthly pension shall be **at least ₱1,300** for members with at least 10 years of service, and **₱2,400** for those with 20 years of service.

### 5-01-04-020 — Retirement Gratuity

**Formula** (RA 1616):

The computation is based on total creditable service converted into **gratuity months**:

| Creditable Year of Service               | Gratuity Months per Year |
| :--------------------------------------- | :----------------------- |
| Not exceeding 20 years                   | **1.0 gratuity month**   |
| Over 20 years but not exceeding 30 years | **1.5 gratuity months**  |
| Over 30 years                            | **2.0 gratuity months**  |

**Total Gratuity**:

```
Gratuity = Total Gratuity Months × Highest Salary Received
```

**Cap**: The gratuity benefit shall **not exceed 24 months**.

**Legal Basis**: RA No. 1616, as amended; DBM guidelines for FY 2025 and onwards.

### 5-01-04-030 — Terminal Leave Benefits

**Formula**:

```
TLB = S × D × CF
```

Where:

- **TLB** = Terminal Leave Benefits
- **S** = Highest monthly salary received
- **D** = Number of accumulated vacation and sick leave credits
- **CF** = Constant Factor = **0.0481927**

**Legal Basis**: CSC Memorandum Circular No. 41, s. 1999; DBM Budget Circular No. 2016-2, dated March 29, 2016.

**Note**: The **constant factor** applies to retirements effective **January 23, 2016 onwards**. An older constant factor of **0.0478087** was used for retirements before that date.

**Example Computation**:

```
TLB = ₱30,000 (highest salary) × 300 (leave credits) × 0.0481927 = ₱433,734.30
```

### 5-01-04-990 — Other Personnel Benefits

**Classification**: **Optional**.

**Coverage**: This account is used for government contributions to employee benefits not falling under any of the specific other personnel accounts. Examples include:

- **Annual Incentive Grant (AIG)**
- Other benefits as may be authorized by law or the President of the Philippines.

## 🏛️ Part VI: PGLU-Specific Considerations

For the **Provincial Government of La Union (PGLU)** , the following specific points apply:

1. **Salary Schedule**: The Third Tranche under EO No. 64, s. 2024 applies to all LGU personnel, whether regular, contractual, or casual. The specific guidelines for LGUs are in **DBM Local Budget Circular No. 172** (July 2026).

2. **RATA Rates**: PGLU officials are covered by **LBC No. 157, s. 2024**. The specific monthly rates for each position (Governor, Vice-Governor, Board Members, Department Heads, etc.) are in **Annex B** of that circular. The rates vary by the LGU's income classification.

3. **CNA Incentive**: PGLU has an existing **Collective Negotiation Agreement (CNA)** , which provides the legal basis for granting a **CNA Incentive** to qualified employees. This is an optional benefit subject to DBM rules and the PS limitation.

4. **PS Limitation**: All optional benefits must comply with the **Personal Services (PS) limitation** under Section 325(a) of RA No. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991). The grant of any optional benefit requires an ordinance from the **Sangguniang Panlalawigan** and is subject to fund availability.

5. **Provident Fund**: If PGLU maintains its own provident or welfare fund, the contribution rate is determined by the fund's by-laws and the LGU's ordinance. The government counterpart contribution is optional and depends on the LGU's decision to maintain such a fund.
